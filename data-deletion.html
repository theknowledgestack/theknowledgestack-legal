User Data Deletion Instructions

The app Tech_2, which we use to publish videos for The Knowledge Stack, does not collect or store personal data about followers, viewers or visitors.

To request deletion of any data related to you

Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → select Tech_2 → Remove.
