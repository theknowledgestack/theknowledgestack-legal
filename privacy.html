Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Knowledge Stack ("we", "us", also known as BeyondTheFeed) runs a short-video channel on Instagram (@theknowledgestack) and a Facebook Page (The Knowledge Stack). To publish our own videos we use a private tool registered with Meta as the app Tech_2 ("the App"). The App is used only by us. Followers, visitors and other people never log in to it or give it any information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, store or process personal information about our followers, viewers, commenters or website visitors. It only handles:

  • Our own Meta access tokens, kept privately on our own computer and used solely to publish content.
  • The videos, captions and hashtags we create and publish.
  • Status information returned by Meta after publishing (for example a video ID and publishing status).

How we use information

Only to publish, schedule and manage our own Reels on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone. Data is exchanged only with Meta (Facebook and Instagram) through their official APIs to publish our content. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Storage and retention

Data stays on our own device. Access can be revoked at any time from our Facebook account settings.

Your rights and data deletion

Because the App holds no data about other people, there is normally nothing to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will remove it. See Data Deletion.

Children

Our content is general-audience, but the App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
